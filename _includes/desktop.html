Summer housing

A brownstone base for a Boston summer. Doubles, triples and quads from May 31 to August 15. Walk to the Esplanade, Copley, Fenway and MIT, then cook dinner on six burners with the other residents.

  • Move in May 31, after 5 PM
  • Move out August 15
  • Copley T station, a short walk
  • Harvard Bridge to campus by bike
